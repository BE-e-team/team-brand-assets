THE PROJECT

Hornet nest detection solution

The yellow legged hornet is an invasive species native to a large portion of Asia. Their population is currently growing rapidly and uncontrollably in a large portion of Continental Europe. They commonly prey on smaller insects, including our precious bees. For this reason our team is working on an efficient solution for finding their nests.
